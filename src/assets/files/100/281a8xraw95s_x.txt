Starscourge Radahn (also called General Radahn) is
a fictional character from the 2022 
action role-playing
video game 
Elden Ring developed by 
FromSoftware. A
demigod, he functions as both a prominent figure in
the history of the game's 
fictional universe, the Lands
Between, and one of its main 
bosses. Radahn is first
encountered in Caelid, a region that has been
consumed by the Scarlet Rot toxin.
"Conqueror of the Stars", Radahn was one of the
world's most powerful warriors, halting the 
destiny
guiding stars themselves in place using gravitational
magic. During the Shattering, an immense 
war of
succession, Radahn battled his half sister 
Malenia to a
standstill in the Caelid wilds. Malenia, close to defeat,
unleashed her full power, spreading Scarlet Rot that
eventually engulfed all of Caelid. Radahn himself lost
his sanity to the Rot and became a mindless, man
eating monster, causing his soldiers to create the
Radahn Festival to attempt to kill him and preserve his
honor.
Defeating Radahn, while optional, is required for the
player to obtain one of the game's Great Runes, shards
of the Elden Ring necessary to progress the storyline.
It is also needed for the optional 
Ranni the
Witch, and to enter the 
quest of 
Shadow of the Erdtree DLC.
Within the DLC, he is fought again at full strength as
Promised Consort Radahn after being resurrected in a
different demigod's body.
